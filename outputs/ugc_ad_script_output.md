# UGC-Style Advertisement Script — Glow Studio

## Campaign Objective

Introduce Glow Studio and encourage potential customers to explore its beauty and personal-care services.

## Platform

Instagram Reel

## Target Audience

People in Hyderabad interested in beauty, grooming, makeup, skincare, and self-care.

## Video Duration

Approximately 25–30 seconds.

## Script

### Scene 1: Hook (0–4 seconds)

**Visual:** A person preparing to get ready in front of a mirror.

**Dialogue:** "Need a little refresh for your next occasion?"

**On-screen text:** Your next beauty refresh starts here.

### Scene 2: Introduce the Brand (4–8 seconds)

**Visual:** Show the Glow Studio name and suitable salon-related visuals.

**Dialogue:** "Let me introduce you to the Glow Studio concept!"

**On-screen text:** Discover Glow Studio.

### Scene 3: Highlight Services (8–19 seconds)

**Visual:** Show suitable visuals representing hair styling, makeup, skincare, and nail care.

**Dialogue:** "Whether you're exploring a new hairstyle, makeup, skincare, or nail care, find inspiration for your personal style."

**On-screen text:** Hair | Makeup | Skincare | Nail Care

### Scene 4: Closing CTA (19–30 seconds)

**Visual:** Show the brand name and website.

**Dialogue:** "Explore the services and find an option that suits you. Take a little time for yourself!"

**On-screen text:** Explore Our Services | Enquire Today

## Tone and Style

* Natural and conversational.
* Friendly and relatable.
* Focused on customer needs.
* Suitable for a short social media video.

## Production Notes

Use authentic-looking visuals, readable captions, and clear audio. Do not present fictional customer testimonials as genuine experiences.

**Project Note:** This is a proposed UGC-style advertisement script for a sample business, not a real customer testimonial.
