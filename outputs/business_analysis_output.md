# Business Analysis — Glow Studio

## 1. Business Overview

**Business Name:** Glow Studio
**Business Type:** Beauty Salon
**Location:** Hyderabad, Telangana
**Project:** AI Content Marketing Using UGC Ads

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept offering beauty and personal-care services. This project explores how AI-generated user-generated content (UGC) advertisements can promote its services across digital platforms.

## 2. Target Audience

* Young adults interested in beauty and skincare.
* Working professionals looking for salon services.
* Customers interested in makeup, hair styling, and skincare.
* Social media users who discover beauty services through short-form videos.

## 3. Marketing Goals

* Increase awareness of Glow Studio.
* Highlight salon services and their benefits.
* Encourage enquiries and appointment bookings.
* Create engaging promotional content for social media.

## 4. Brand Voice

The brand voice should be friendly, professional, relatable, confident, and welcoming.

## 5. Content Strategy

* Use relatable customer scenarios.
* Start advertisements with attention-grabbing hooks.
* Focus on customer needs and service benefits.
* Include clear calls to action.
* Adapt content to the requirements of different social media platforms.

## 6. AI Tool

Google Gemini can be used to generate and refine marketing ideas, hooks, platform-specific content, and UGC-style advertisement scripts.

## 7. Review Process

AI-generated content should be reviewed for accuracy, originality, tone, and suitability before publication.

**Project Note:** Glow Studio is used as an example business for this learning project.
