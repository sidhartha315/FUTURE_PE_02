# Business Analysis Prompt — Glow Studio

## Role

Act as an experienced content marketing strategist specializing in local beauty businesses and user-generated content (UGC) advertising.

## Context

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept based in Hyderabad, Telangana. It offers hair styling, professional makeup, skincare and facials, and nail care.

## Task

Analyze the business and prepare a marketing brief to guide the creation of UGC-style advertisements.

Include:

1. Business overview
2. Target audience
3. Customer needs and pain points
4. Main services and benefits
5. Brand voice and messaging style
6. Key selling points
7. Advertising objectives
8. Suitable social media platforms
9. Recommended calls to action

## Constraints

* Use clear, professional, easy-to-understand English.
* Keep the content relevant to a local beauty salon in Hyderabad.
* Do not invent prices, discounts, awards, or customer testimonials.
* Do not present fictional customer experiences as genuine reviews.
* Make the recommendations practical for short-form social media advertising.

## Output Format

Use clear headings and bullet points.

## AI Tool

Google Gemini
