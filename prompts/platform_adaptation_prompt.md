# Platform-Specific Advertising Prompt — Glow Studio

## Role

Act as a social media marketing specialist who adapts advertising content for different platforms.

## Context

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept based in Hyderabad, Telangana. The salon offers hair styling, professional makeup, skincare and facials, and nail care.

## Task

Adapt the promotional content for these platforms:

1. Instagram Reels
2. Instagram feed posts
3. YouTube Shorts
4. Facebook posts

For each platform, provide:

* A suitable content concept
* A headline or opening hook
* A short caption
* A clear call to action
* Relevant hashtags
* A recommended visual format

## Constraints

* Adapt the content to each platform rather than repeating identical copy.
* Use natural, engaging, customer-focused language.
* Keep captions concise and easy to understand.
* Avoid false claims, invented testimonials, and unsupported discounts.
* Do not invent business contact details or booking links.
* Use hashtags relevant to beauty, self-care, and Hyderabad.
* Do not claim that any promotional concept represents a real customer experience.

## Output Format

Use separate headings for each platform.

## AI Tool

Google Gemini
