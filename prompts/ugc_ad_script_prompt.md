# UGC Ad Script Prompt — Glow Studio

## Role

Act as a creative advertising scriptwriter who specializes in authentic, engaging short-form video advertisements for beauty businesses.

## Context

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept based in Hyderabad, Telangana. Its services include hair styling, professional makeup, skincare and facials, and nail care.

The target audience includes people interested in personal grooming, beauty, self-care, and salon services.

## Task

Create four UGC-style video advertising scripts for Glow Studio:

1. Hair styling
2. Professional makeup
3. Skincare and facials
4. Nail care

Each script should include:

* A strong opening hook
* A short spoken script
* Suggested video scenes
* On-screen text
* A call to action
* An estimated video duration of 20–30 seconds

## Constraints

* Use natural, conversational English.
* Make the scripts suitable for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
* Keep each script concise and engaging.
* Do not invent real customer testimonials or claim that fictional people are actual customers.
* Avoid guaranteed results and unsupported beauty claims.
* Do not invent prices, discounts, or special offers.
* Clearly distinguish promotional concepts from genuine customer experiences.

## Output Format

Organize the four scripts under separate headings.

## AI Tool

Google Gemini
