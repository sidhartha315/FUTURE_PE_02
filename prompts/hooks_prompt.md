# UGC Advertising Hooks Prompt — Glow Studio

## Role

Act as a social media content strategist who specializes in engaging short-form video advertisements for beauty and skincare businesses.

## Context

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept based in Hyderabad, Telangana. Its services include hair styling, professional makeup, skincare and facials, and nail care.

The target audience includes people interested in beauty, self-care, skincare, makeup, and salon services.

## Task

Generate 15 attention-grabbing hooks for UGC-style advertisements promoting Glow Studio.

Organize the hooks into these categories:

1. Problem-based hooks — 3
2. Curiosity-driven hooks — 3
3. Benefit-focused hooks — 3
4. Relatable lifestyle hooks — 3
5. Question-based hooks — 3

## Constraints

* Keep each hook short, natural, and easy to speak on camera.
* Use conversational English suitable for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
* Make the hooks relatable to the target audience in Hyderabad.
* Avoid exaggerated beauty claims and guaranteed results.
* Do not invent customer testimonials or personal experiences.
* Avoid misleading statements, false urgency, and unsupported discounts.
* Make every hook different from the others.

## Output Format

For each hook, provide:

* Category
* Hook
* Suggested visual opening

## AI Tool

Google Gemini
